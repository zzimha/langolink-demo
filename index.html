What's different

Built around what you want to say, not a streak.

Most apps teach you words in a fixed order. LangoLink starts from the situation in front of you.

1

You choose the moment

Kopitiam order, a work small talk, a trip next month. You pick it, and the conversation follows you.

2

Private first, people second

Practise with an AI partner where nobody is watching, then move to a real learner from another country when you are ready.

3

Feedback on your own sentences

Not a quiz score. You get what sounded good, what to fix, and a more natural way to say it.

4

No streak guilt

Progress is counted in conversations you have had. Miss a week and nothing is lost or reset.
